# Coffee and chair references

The cabin recipe is an original, adjustable starting point for a light-roasted Gesha: 18 g / 300 g, a 60 g bloom and gentle pours. It is not a claimed universal recipe for every roast. References were [Partners Coffee's Gesha V60 guide](https://www.partnerscoffee.com/blogs/education/brew-guides-edin-lopez-gesha) for a light-roast starting point and [Hario's one-cup V60 guide](https://www.hario-usa.com/blogs/recipes-and-more-from-friends/james-hoffmann-1-cup-v60-technique) for bloom/pour structure. The in-world coffee animation compresses the real brewing time.

The desk chair is original procedural geometry inspired by the open mesh, curved frame, armrests and caster base of the [Herman Miller Aeron](https://www.hermanmiller.com/products/seating/office-chairs/aeron-chair/). No manufacturer model or texture was copied. The seat and back use actual open line geometry.

The packing list and book margin are ordinary cabin details. They do not participate in either password puzzle.

The pink illustrated Gesha recipe was supplied by Brydon. The original PNG is used unchanged as the paper texture and enlarged recipe view.

The coffee interaction is a one-cup ritual per page load: grind, dose the V60, wait for the hearth kettle to boil, take it off the flames, fill the gooseneck, then pour from either the gooseneck or V60. Animated work pauses in hidden tabs; leaving the room mutes the coffee sounds. Fire cancels the ritual. Noise sources reuse the existing prewarmed audio buffers. Screen-reader descriptions report the same equipment state shown by the models without adding a visual checklist.
