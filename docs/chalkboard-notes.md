# Chalkboard research notes

The board is authored in `js/chalkboard.mjs` and drawn once as a canvas texture on the existing solid chalkboard. Clicking the board opens the same drawing at reading size. These are working diagrams, not reproduced publication figures or numerical data.

- **NMR / zeolites:** Brouwer, Vanderhout, Hurst and Eastman, [From Solid-State NMR to Crystal Structures through Combinatorial Tiling Theory](https://doi.org/10.1107/S0108767318099063), and its abstract in `content/research.html`. The honeycomb is explicitly a Euclidean {6,3} toy example, not a claimed zeolite structure. Its vertex–edge-midpoint–face-center barycentric chambers have one orbit under the full symmetry group, fixed neighbor maps, m01=6 and m12=3. Conventions follow [EPINET's Delaney–Dress introduction](https://epinet.anu.edu.au/page/epinet2_mathematics_delaney_dress). The four illustrative NMR peaks are decorative, not measured chemical shifts.
- **Circulants:** Vander Meulen, Van Tuyl and Watt, [Cohen–Macaulay Circulant Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.8351), Figure 1. The 12 vertices have modular steps 1, 3 and 4, drawn in three chalk colors. The board poses Cohen–Macaulayness as a question; it does not assert that the illustrated graph has the property. The paper explicitly thanks Brydon for the LaTeX graph-drawing code.
- **Companion matrix / digraph:** the non-Fiedler 4×4 example and intercyclic construction from Brydon's [research summary](https://brydon.ai/research.html), discussing [Characterization of Companion Matrices](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0024379514005953). Rows are (0,1,0,0), (0,0,1,0), (−a3,0,−a1,1), (−a4,0,−a2,0). Matrix entry Cij represents i→j. The coefficient block is rows 3–4, columns 1–3. Every directed cycle passes through vertex 3; the characteristic polynomial is λ⁴+a1λ³+a2λ²+a3λ+a4.
- **Chemostat:** Bolger, Eastman, Hill and Wolkowicz, [A Predator-Prey Model in the Chemostat with Holling Type II Response Function](https://ojs.lib.uwo.ca/index.php/mase/article/download/10842/11065), equations (2.5), Proposition 4.1 and the global analysis. The board uses the scaled model, q(x)=ax/(b+x), and an explicitly schematic supercritical Hopf branch, with solid stable equilibria/cycle extrema and a dashed unstable equilibrium. The phase sketch shows the unique attracting periodic orbit in the unstable-coexistence regime; the equilibrium itself is excluded from the attracting-orbit note. The sketch is qualitative and has no invented numerical parameter values.
